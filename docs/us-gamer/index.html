01 / YOUR GAMING IDENTITY

A name your
community knows.

Your U.S. Gamer Network Tag connects your gaming identity to event check-in and tagged Solana transactions.

  1. 01

    Save your Network Tag

    Set your U.S. Gamer Event Network Gamertag in VisaUSD before the event. Use the appropriate network or event prefix with your gaming name.

  2. 02

    Verify at event check-in

    U.S. Gamer organizers manually verify your tag against your logged-in gaming account when you check in.

  3. 03

    Use it in your event history

    With a tag saved, eligible VisaUSD Solana transfers automatically include it in the transaction’s public Memo. Review the memo before you approve the transfer.

02 / WRITTEN TO SOLANA

Your event record.
Carried forward.

U.S. Gamer Event Records use the Solana Memo field to preserve the context of tagged transactions.

With your Network Tag saved, VisaUSD adds it to eligible Solana transfers. When both participants’ tags are available, the memo records the sender, recipient, amount and asset.

Once finalized on Solana, these transactions form immutable U.S. Gamer Event Records. Their original record cannot be edited, carrying the event history forward.
