# Asset provenance

The 51 recordings were supplied in the user's ATV Radio folder. VisaUSD™ logo artwork is packaged as `web/assets/visausd-logomark.png`. Their owners retain their existing rights. These assets are included for the requested VisaUSD™ Radio resource and demo; the software's MIT license does not grant separate rights to the recordings, names, or artwork.

`config/source-audio.json` records source filenames used for conversion. `config/playlist.json` records packaged asset names, durations, metadata, and SHA-256 checksums. The user assigned `Digitaldruglord & Spynx` to the 32 songs that initially had no artist metadata. Other supplied artist credits are retained. Displayed credits live in the playlist manifest; source recordings and their original tags remain unchanged.

No GTA V texture dictionary or other Rockstar game artwork is distributed. A server operator prepares the native wheel atlas using their supported game build.

The Vice City sunset background was supplied by the user and is packaged unchanged as `web/assets/vice-city-sunset.png`. It retains its existing rights.
